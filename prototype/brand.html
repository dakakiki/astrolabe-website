Logo proposal · October 2026

The name is an instrument.

For two thousand years the astrolabe was how astronomers and astrologers found where the stars stood for a given moment and place — exactly what AstroLabe does for every client. The marks below borrow its parts, in the app's own colours: starlight blue for the sky, old brass for the instrument.

ShackleThe ring the instrument hangs from — what makes a circle read as an astrolabe, not a planet or a clock.
LimbThe brass rim with its degree scale.
ReteThe star map on top: an off-centre ecliptic ring and pointed star markers.
RuleThe bar turned to sight a star or read a time.

Using the mark
Clear space

Keep free space around the mark equal to the shackle ring's height on every side.

Smallest size

24 px for the full mark; below that, the simplified favicon on its night tile.

Colour

Night version on dark, day version on light, one-colour version for print, stamps and engraving.

Wordmark

“AstroLabe” in Newsreader Medium, with the capital L — as in the app's name.

Don't

Recolour the star, rotate the rule, add effects or place the night version on a light photo.
