Privacy & security

Your clients trust you with their birth data. We treat it that way.

Birth details, notes and files are handled with the care you would give health records — in how they are stored, logged and exported.

Hosted in the EU

Your practice lives on servers in the European Union, on its own machine — never next to a marketing website.

A GDPR agreement included

You are the controller of your clients' data, we are the processor — written down in a data processing agreement you accept at sign-up.

Two-factor sign-in

Turn on an authenticator app, and every new sign-in needs a code from your phone.

Encrypted nightly backups

Every night, encrypted before it leaves the server and kept for 14 days — with restores tested, not just promised.

Take everything with you

Export your whole practice — clients, charts, notes, files and payments — as one download, whenever you want.

Delete means delete

When a client asks to be forgotten, their data is erased — not hidden behind a flag.

Questions

Good to know

Does AstroLabe replace my chart software?

For everyday client work it often can: natal charts, transits, synastry, composite charts and the sky calendar are all built in, next to the client they belong to.

It does not write interpretations, and some techniques — progressions and solar returns, for example — are not there yet. Tell us which ones you need; the beta decides what comes next.

Which traditions does it support?

Western, Vedic, Hellenistic and the schools built on them. You choose the zodiac, ayanamsa, house system and orbs for your practice, and can change them for any single chart.

Can my clients sign in?

Not yet. AstroLabe is your private workspace; clients never see it. A client portal with online booking is planned after the beta.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export your whole practice at any time. If you close your account, you have 30 days to change your mind; after that everything is permanently deleted, backups included once they expire.

Which languages?

English for now. More languages are planned — tell us yours when you request an invite.

What will it cost after the beta?

The plans we have in mind start at €19 a month, or €190 a year. Prices are confirmed with beta members before any paid plan opens, and nobody is moved to a paid plan without asking.
